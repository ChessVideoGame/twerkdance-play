Play Twerk Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Publisher: Ai-Blockchain, Inc. ("AIB", "we", "us")

Contact: Ai-Blockchain, Inc., 50 Harrison Street, Suite 212B, Hoboken, NJ 07030

This policy covers the Play Twerk game on Android (and later iOS and other stores if we publish there), and the website playtwerk.com, together called "the Game".

The short version

We don't collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no in-app purchases in the current build. The mobile app is not designed to send your personal data to our servers.

Information we collect

None. We don't ask for, collect, store, or receive your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device advertising IDs, or gameplay data for profiling.

Age and content

Play Twerk is intended for adults (18+) and features suggestive dance content. It is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by mail), contact us at the address above and we'll delete it.

Playing on playtwerk.com

When you visit playtwerk.com, your browser requests pages and files from our website host. Like any website, that request can include technical information such as your IP address. We don't use hosting logs to identify, profile, or track players for advertising. The marketing site uses no analytics or tracking pixels that we configure for advertising.

App stores

If you download or buy the Game from Google Play, the Apple App Store, the Amazon Appstore, or Epic Games Store, the store may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example downloads and payments). We don't receive your personal information from those stores for advertising.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information. We don't share personal information for targeted advertising, because we don't collect it. We use no third-party ad or analytics SDKs in the current Play Twerk build.

If you contact us

If you write to us by mail about privacy, we'll use your letter only to respond and will keep it only as long as needed to handle the request, unless the law requires longer retention.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states, the EU/EEA, or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to use of personal information. Because the Game doesn't collect personal information, we usually won't hold data about you. You can still contact us at the address above. EU/UK residents may also complain to their local data protection authority.

Security

Because we keep no personal data from gameplay, there is very little player data to protect. Mail we receive is handled with reasonable care.

Changes

If we change how the Game handles information, we'll update this policy and the effective date before the change takes effect, and we'll update Google Play (and other store) privacy disclosures. If we ever start collecting personal information, we'll explain what and why here first, and get consent where the law requires it.
